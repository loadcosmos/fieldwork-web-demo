Employer: Northwind Labs. Your guide to health coverage, retirement savings and everyday support. Plan year: January 1 through December 31, 2026.

This fictional guide is for demonstration only. Benefits are available to eligible US employees working at least 30 hours per week. Elections generally take effect on the first of the month after your start date.

Medical / HSA

Choose between a preferred provider organization (PPO) plan and a qualifying high-deductible health plan (HDHP). Both include preventive care with in-network providers and access to telehealth appointments.

For eligible employees enrolled in the HDHP, the employer contributes $1,000 to your HSA each year. Contributions are paid in equal installments during the year. You must satisfy HSA eligibility requirements to receive contributions.

Your HSA belongs to you, including when you change jobs. Your account provider may offer investment options, subject to a minimum cash balance. Investment returns are not guaranteed.

Health care FSA

The health care FSA reimburses eligible out-of-pocket medical, dental and vision expenses. Elect your annual contribution during enrollment; midyear changes generally require a qualifying life event.

Under the health FSA, you may carry over up to $680 of unused funds to the next plan year. There is no grace period. Expenses for the current plan year must be incurred by December 31, 2026.

Keep itemized receipts and explanations of benefits. A card payment may still require documentation. Contact the plan administrator if you need help substantiating an expense.

Dental and vision

Dental coverage includes two preventive cleanings per year and a network of participating dentists. Vision coverage includes an annual eye examination and an allowance toward prescription glasses or contact lenses. Consult the carrier guide for exclusions and network details.

401(k) retirement savings

Northwind Labs matches 100% of the first 6% of eligible pay that you contribute to the 401(k) plan. Employer matching contributions are immediately vested. Change your payroll election through the retirement provider portal.

After-tax contributions are allowed in addition to regular employee deferrals. In-plan Roth conversion is available for eligible after-tax balances, sometimes called a mega backdoor Roth strategy. Plan and federal contribution limits apply.

Pre-tax and designated Roth deferrals share an annual employee limit. Review your payroll deductions after salary changes. Contact the plan administrator to confirm processing times and available contribution types.

Perks and reimbursements

Wellness reimbursement up to $600 per calendar year covers eligible gym memberships, fitness classes and approved wellbeing programs. Submit itemized receipts showing payment.

A tuition/learning stipend $2,000 per calendar year supports job-related courses, books and professional certifications. Obtain manager approval before enrolling in a course.

A home office/ergonomic stipend $300 per calendar year supports an ergonomic chair, desk accessories and approved home office equipment. Equipment must meet the expense policy.

Time away and employee support

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off, 11 company holidays and access to confidential employee assistance counseling. Coordinate leave with your manager so the team can plan coverage.

Deadlines

Perk expenses must be incurred during 2026. Reimbursement claims must be submitted by January 31, 2027. Unused annual stipend balances do not roll over. Keep receipts and allow time for your manager to approve the claim.

For questions, contact People & Culture through the internal benefits portal. This sample summary is illustrative and does not create a real benefits plan.
